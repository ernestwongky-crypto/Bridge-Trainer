# Changi Bridge Trainer

OOW crossing-vessel drill on the real track-brief legs: A (South of CNB-3 By) → B on 180, 1.4 nm; B → C on 249, 7.5 nm, westbound ITZ.
One leg per scenario at 12 kn, one contact at a time; the drill skips ahead between contacts and ends at the wheel-over.
Lookout, nav, helm and CO speak; voice orders via the browser microphone, or Voice call (Deepgram + Claude).

Geography is approximate and not for navigation.
