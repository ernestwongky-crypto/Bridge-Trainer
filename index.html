Your margins: CPA 2.5 cables, bow crossing 8 cables. One contact at a time: lookout calls up and waits for "OOW", nav follows straight on, then it is your call. Nothing else is reported until the ship is steady on your ordered course (helm: "Course …"), you order a speed, or you give a sitrep ("Watch-on-deck, sitrep … Watch-on-deck") whose intention is to maintain course and speed. "Carry on" or tapping the status bar also moves on. Say "check port / starboard / stern" for the lookout, "check two five nine" for nav's blind check, "nav" for a follow-up, "lookout" for bearing movement, "nav bow crossing range", "check starboard, check zero one zero", then declare it clear and give the order, e.g. "starboard clear, two five nine clear, starboard fifteen, slow ahead both". Engine orders: dead slow / slow / half / full ahead, stop both (half ahead = 12 kn).
